How this detector works

  1. Both inputs are normalized identically: lowercase, punctuation removal, and whitespace cleanup.
  2. Meaningful sentences/passages from Document B become patterns.
  3. Every pattern is searched in Document A using Naïve and KMP independently.
  4. The overlap score is the percentage of normalized Document B covered by patterns found in A.

Complexity comparison

AlgorithmWorst caseExtra space
NaïveO(n × m)O(1)

Why KMP helps: the Longest Proper Prefix/Suffix (LPS) table prevents rechecking characters after a mismatch.

Important: This is an educational exact-overlap indicator, not a legal plagiarism verdict. It intentionally does not score paraphrases or semantic similarity, because the project demonstrates classical string matching.
